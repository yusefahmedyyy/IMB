The chef

From Chasa Montana and the Burgtheater to a kitchen of his own.

Alexander trained under the family behind Rahofer, cooked at Relais & Châteaux Chasa Montana in Switzerland and at Vestibül in the Vienna Burgtheater under Christian Domschitz, then led the kitchens at Meineck, Taborturm and finally Rahofer, a house that was a genuine candidate for a third Gault&Millau toque.
